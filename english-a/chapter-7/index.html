Chapter 7 question answers

7 questions with answers from the 9th class English textbook, typed word for word from the class notes.

1.According to the poet, how should one respond to criticism and blame?

Ans: The poet advises to keep one's head and not give in to blame or criticism.

2. What quality does the poem emphasize in handling doubt and self-trust?

Ans: The poem emphasizes the quality of self-trust and making allowance for others' doubts.

3. What message does the poem give about dreams and aspirations?

The poem advises not to make dreams one's master, but to have dreams without being controlled by them.

4.How should a person handle twisted truths and personal loses ?

The poem advises to bear the twisted truths and rebuild after personal losses with worn-out tools.

5. What does the poem suggest about resilience and rebuilding after setbacks?

Ans: The poem suggests that one should rebuild after setbacks, even with worn-out tools, and not give up.

6. According to the poem, how should one persist in difficult times?

Ans: The poem advises to hold on with the Will, even when there is nothing left in you.

7. What is the message of the poem regarding integrity and humility in life?

Ans: The poem advises to maintain integrity and humility in life, by keeping virtue while talking with crowds and walking with kings.
