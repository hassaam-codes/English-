1.what is globalisation and how does it promote economic growth?

Globalization is the interconnectedness of world economies, societies, and cultures. It promotes economic growth through increased trade, foreign investment, and economies of scale.

2.How has globalisation impacted Pakistan's economy in terms of trade and investment?

Globalization has increased Pakistan's trade and investment through expanded exports, foreign direct investment, and remittances, but also exposed it to global market fluctuations and competition.

Q3. What are some of the new job opportunities created in Pakistan as a result of globalisation?

Ans: Some new job opportunities created in Pakistan due to globalization include:

1. IT and software development jobs

2. Call center and customer service jobs

3. Export-oriented manufacturing jobs

4. Logistics and supply chain management jobs

5. Freelance writing, designing, and consulting jobs

6. E-commerce and online marketing jobs

7. International business and trade jobs

8. Tourism and hospitality jobs

Q4. What is cultural exchange, and how does it benefit people?

Ans: Cultural exchange is sharing and learning different cultures, promoting understanding, tolerance, and broadening perspectives.

Q5: What concerns are raised about the preservation of traditional Pakistani culture in the context of globalisation?

Several concerns are raised about the preservation of traditional Pakistani culture in the context of globalization,

including:

1.Loss of cultural identity .

2.Erosion of traditional values .

3. Western cultural dominance

4. Cultural homogenization

5. Decline of traditional arts and crafts

Q6. How has globalisation expanded access to information for students?

Globalization has expanded access to information for students through the internet, online resources, digital libraries, and online educational platforms, making it easier to access knowledge and connect with peers worldwide.

Q7.How does learning about diverse perspectives benefit students?

Learning about diverse perspectives benefits students by broadening their understanding, promoting empathy, challenging assumptions, and preparing them for a globalized world.

Q8. How can understanding globalisation help the young Pakistani students contributing to their country's future?

Ans: Understanding globalization can help young Pakistani students contribute to their country's future by developing skills, knowledge, and perspectives that enable them to innovate, compete, and collaborate in a globalized economy.
