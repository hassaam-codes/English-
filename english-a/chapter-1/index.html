Chapter 1 question answers

8 questions with answers from the 9th class English textbook, typed word for word from the class notes.

1. How might the geographical description of Makkah Mukarramah influence the reader's understanding of its historical cultural importance?

Ans: The geographical description of Makkah Mukarramah significantly influences reader's understanding of its historical and cultural importance.

2. What does the emphasis on the Arabs' memory and eloquence suggest about their culture before Islam?

Ans: The emphasis on Arabs' memory and eloquence before Islam suggests that their culture heavily relied on oral tradition, where information, history, stories, and even law were passed down primarily by memorization.

3. What is the importance of the anecdote about Hammad and Caliph Walid bin Yazid?

Ans: The anecdote about Hammad and Caliph Walid bin Yazid is significant because it highlights the Arabs' sharp memory, poetical skills and rich culture.

4. What parallels can you draw between the state of civilization described here and the other historical periods of decline and renewal?

Ans: The description of civilization on the verge of chaos resembles other historical periods of decline and renewal; the fall of Roman Empire which gave rise to Christianity and Islam.

5. What challenges did the Rasoolullah face in proclaiming the Oneness of Allah and the unity of mankind?

Ans: The Rasoolullah pbuh faced opposition and persecution from people who didn't want to give up their old beliefs and traditions.

6. In what ways did the Rasoolullah pbuh elevate mankind to a higher level in both spiritual and worldly domains?

Ans: The Rasoolullah pbuh elevated mankind kind to a higher level in both spiritual and worldly domains by introducing the teachings of Islam, which emphasized monotheism.

7. How does the final Quranic verse (Quran 33:45-46) encapsulate the role and mission of the Rasoolullah pbuh?

Ans: The final verse of Surah Al-Ahzab (Quran 33:45-46) encapsulates the role of the Rasoolullah pbuh as a witness by highlighting its comprehensive mission to deliver Allah's message.

8 How does the author view the pre-Islamic Arabs? Write down the examples and the evidences supporting your inferences.

Ans: The author views the pre-Islamic Arabs as ignorant, superstitious, and morally corrupt, but also acknowledges their poetic and linguistic abilities.
