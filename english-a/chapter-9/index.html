Q1.How has quality education been defined in the text?

Ans. Quality education has been defined as a holistic approach that equips learners with knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes to thrive in a changing world.

Q2.Name three benefits of quality education mentioned in the essay.

Ans.Three benefits of quality education mentioned in the essay are:

1. Economic growth

2. Social cohesion

3. Innovation

Q3.Why is human capital development important for a country's economic growth and sustainable development?

Ans: Human capital development is important because it enables individuals to accumulate knowledge, skills, and health, contributing to economic growth and sustainable development.

Q4. Describe the role of education in reducing inequality and poverty.

Ans: Education reduces inequality and poverty by providing equal opportunities, promoting social mobility, and empowering

individuals with skills and knowledge.

Q 5. How does quality education promote understanding and tolerance?

Ans: Quality education promotes understanding and tolerance by teaching students about different cultures, religions, and perspectives, fostering mutual respect and empathy.

Q6. How does teaching entrepreneurship in schools promote economic growth?

Ans: Teaching entrepreneurship in schools promotes economic growth by fostering innovation, creativity, and job creation, equipping students with skills to start and manage businesses.

Q7. Why is STEM education important for driving innovation?

Ans: STEM education is important for driving innovation because it . equips students with technical skills, critical thinking, and. problem-solving abilities, fostering curiosity and creativity.

Q8 . Can you think of any ways to improve the quality of education in your own school or community?

Ans : Investing in teacher training, updating curriculum, and providing access to technology and resources can improve the quality of education in schools and communities.
