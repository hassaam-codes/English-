1. How had Taseer wasted his time before he visited his grandfather?

Ans. Instead of doing his schoolwork and chores, Taseer had wasted his time in playing video games, chatting with friends, and daydreaming.

2. In what way did the poor time management affect Taseer's performance?

Ans. Poor Time Management

Because of poor time management, Taseer had to rush his assignments, and some incomplete tasks. His teachers and parents were concerned about him.

3. How did taseer fill the jar?

Ans. Taseer Filling the Jar

Firstly, Taseer put some sand in the jar. Then he put the pebbles. Lastly, he tried to fit the big rocks, but they didn't fit properly in the jar.

4. Why did his grandfather decide to help Taseer?

Ans. Grandfather helping Taseer:

His grandfather decided to help Taseer because he had noticed that Taseer was struggling with time management and wanted to teach him a valuable lesson.

5. What is the impact of accurate time management on Taseer's performance?

Ans. Impact of Accurate Time Management:

Because of accurate time management, Taseer starts to see improvements. He feels more in control of his time and less stressed about his time. His grades are improved, and he has more time to spend with his family and friends.
