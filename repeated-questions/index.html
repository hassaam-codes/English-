2. Change the voice sentences (Q.9) — repeated

  1. The dog has not been beaten by her.

    Answer She has not beaten the dog.

    4 times: 2023 Group I, 2024 Group I, 2024 Group II, 2026 Group II

  2. Why is he mending the chair?

    Answer Why is the chair being mended by him?

    4 times: 2023 Group I, 2025 Group II, 2026 Group I, 2026 Group II

  3. A letter will be written by him.

    ⇄

    He will write a letter.

    Answer He will write a letter.

    Answer A letter will be written by him.

    3 times: 2022 Group I, 2023 Group II, 2024 Group II

  4. We shall have finished our work by March next.

    Answer Our work will have been finished by us by March next.

    3 times: 2022 Group II, 2025 Group II, 2026 Group II

  5. The picture is made by the boy.

    ⇄

    The boy makes the picture.

    Answer The boy makes the picture.

    Answer The picture is made by the boy.

    3 times: 2023 Group I, 2024 Group II, 2025 Group II

  6. She gave me five films.

    ⇄

    I was given five films by her.

    Answer I was given five films by her.

    Answer She gave me five films.

    3 times: 2024 Group I, 2026 Group I, 2026 Group II

  7. Why did she write such a letter?

    Answer Why was such a letter written by her?

    2 times: 2022 Group I, 2023 Group II

  8. The boy is climbing the wall.

    Answer The wall is being climbed by the boy.

    2 times: 2022 Group I, 2023 Group II

  9. They have bought a horse.

    Answer A horse has been bought by them.

    2 times: 2022 Group II, 2023 Group II

  10. He will give you a box of chocolate.

    ⇄

    He will give you a box of chocolates.

    Answer You will be given a box of chocolate by him.

    Answer You will be given a box of chocolates by him.

    2 times: 2022 Group II, 2023 Group I

  11. The house is being bought by them.

    Answer They are buying the house.

    2 times: 2022 Group II, 2023 Group II

  12. They are buying this house.

    Answer This house is being bought by them.

    2 times: 2023 Group I, 2024 Group II

  13. The snake will have been killed by us.

    Answer We shall have killed the snake.

    2 times: 2024 Group I, 2025 Group I

  14. The door of the car was opened by the driver.

    ⇄

    The driver opened the door of the car.

    Answer The driver opened the door of the car.

    Answer The door of the car was opened by the driver.

    2 times: 2025 Group I, 2025 Group II

  15. She likes apples.

    Answer Apples are liked by her.

    2 times: 2025 Group I, 2026 Group I

  16. The Board has given me a gold medal.

    Answer I have been given a gold medal by the Board.

    2 times: 2025 Group I, 2025 Group II
