Q.8 Translate the following sentences into English

  1. پاکستان میرا پیارا وطن ہے۔

    Answer Pakistan is my dear homeland.

  2. وہ اب کہاں ہے؟

    Answer Where is he now?

  3. آپ ہر روز نہاتے ہیں۔

    Answer You take a bath every day.

  4. میں چائے نہیں بنا رہی ہوں۔

    Answer I am not making tea.

  5. میں ناشتہ کر چکا ہوں۔

    Answer I have had my breakfast.

  6. بچے شور نہیں کر رہے ہیں۔

    Answer The children are not making a noise.

  7. دوپہر کے وقت آرام کیا جاتا ہے۔

    Answer Rest is taken at noon.

  8. طالب علم چار ماہ سے محنت کر رہا ہے۔

    Answer The student has been working hard for four months.

Q.9 Change the voice

  1. The dog has not been beaten by her.

    Answer She has not beaten the dog.

  2. Why is he mending the chair?

    Answer Why is the chair being mended by him?

  3. I was given five films by her.

    Answer She gave me five films.

  4. We shall have finished our work by March next.

    Answer Our work will have been finished by us by March next.

  5. He had told me to do it.

    Answer I had been told by him to do it.
