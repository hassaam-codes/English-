Q.8 Translate the following sentences into English

  1. کیا بچہ شام سے سوتا رہا ہو گا؟

    Answer Will the child have been sleeping since evening?

  2. میں اسے معاف نہیں کروں گا۔

    Answer I shall not forgive him.

  3. 1943ء سے پہلے جنگ لڑی نہیں جا چکی تھی۔

    Answer The war had not been fought before 1943.

  4. کیا تمام کتابیں جلا دی گئیں؟

    Answer Were all the books burnt?

  5. کیا لڑکے ایک گھنٹے سے کھیل رہے تھے؟

    Answer Had the boys been playing for an hour?

  6. تم نے کس سے مشورہ کیا؟

    Answer Whom did you consult?

  7. دوپہر کے وقت آرام کیا جاتا ہے۔

    Answer Rest is taken at noon.

  8. امجد انعام حاصل کر چکا ہے۔

    Answer Amjad has won the prize.

Q.9 Change the voice

  1. The mother loves the children.

    Answer The children are loved by the mother.

  2. She gave me five films.

    Answer I was given five films by her.

  3. The dog has not been beaten by her.

    Answer She has not beaten the dog.

  4. The snake will have been killed by us.

    Answer We shall have killed the snake.

  5. A car ran over an old man.

    Answer An old man was run over by a car.
