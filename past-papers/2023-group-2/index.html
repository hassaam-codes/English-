Q.8 Translate the following sentences into English

  1. سورج غروب ہو چکا ہو گا۔

    Answer The sun will have set.

  2. وہ مجھے ملنے آئے گا۔

    Answer He will come to see me.

  3. وہ ایک گھنٹے سے خط لکھ رہا تھا۔

    Answer He had been writing a letter for an hour.

  4. میں نے ایک کیمرہ خریدا۔

    Answer I bought a camera.

  5. میں ناشتہ کر چکا ہوں۔

    Answer I have had my breakfast.

  6. وہ اپنے گناہ پر نہیں پچھتاتا ہے۔

    Answer He does not repent of his sin.

  7. میں تیرنا جانتا ہوں۔

    Answer I know how to swim.

  8. لوگوں کو خبر ہونے سے پہلے آگ بجھائی جا چکی تھی۔

    Answer The fire had been put out before the people came to know about it.

Q.9 Change the voice

  1. A letter will be written by him.

    Answer He will write a letter.

  2. The boy is climbing the wall.

    Answer The wall is being climbed by the boy.

  3. Why did she write such a letter?

    Answer Why was such a letter written by her?

  4. The house is being bought by them.

    Answer They are buying the house.

  5. They have bought a horse.

    Answer A horse has been bought by them.
