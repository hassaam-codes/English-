Q.8 Translate the following sentences into English

  1. اصغر کے پاس کتنے لٹو تھے؟

    Answer How many tops did Asghar have?

  2. پرچہ حل کرنے میں تقریباً تین گھنٹے لگیں گے۔

    Answer It will take about three hours to solve the paper.

  3. کیا بارش شام سے ہو رہی ہے؟

    Answer Has it been raining since evening?

  4. تم نے اپنا وعدہ پورا نہ کیا۔

    Answer You did not keep your promise.

  5. گھوڑے ریگستان میں نہیں دوڑتے ہیں۔

    Answer Horses do not run in the desert.

  6. کیا فصل کاٹی نہیں جا رہی تھی؟

    Answer Was the crop not being harvested?

  7. فقیر بھیک نہیں مانگ رہا ہو گا۔

    Answer The beggar will not be begging.

  8. پولیس ہجوم کو منتشر کر چکی ہو گی۔

    Answer The police will have dispersed the crowd.

Q.9 Change the voice

  1. She likes apples.

    Answer Apples are liked by her.

  2. Why is he mending the chair?

    Answer Why is the chair being mended by him?

  3. The doctor asked her to stay in bed.

    Answer She was asked by the doctor to stay in bed.

  4. She gave me five films.

    Answer I was given five films by her.

  5. They had gained nothing.

    Answer Nothing had been gained by them.
