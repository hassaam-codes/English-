Q.8 Translate the following sentences into English

  1. لاہور باغات کے لیے مشہور ہے۔

    Answer Lahore is famous for its gardens.

  2. امیر آدمی کے پاس کتنی کاریں ہیں؟

    Answer How many cars does the rich man have?

  3. یہ کپڑا سستا بک رہا ہے۔

    Answer This cloth is selling cheap.

  4. امجد انعام حاصل کر چکا ہے۔

    Answer Amjad has won the prize.

  5. اصغر پندرہ منٹ سے نہا رہا ہے۔

    Answer Asghar has been taking a bath for fifteen minutes.

  6. مسافروں نے جنگل میں آگ لگائی۔

    Answer The travellers lit a fire in the forest.

  7. ہماری فوج شہروں کو تباہ نہیں کرے گی۔

    Answer Our army will not destroy the cities.

  8. غریب ڈاکیا بڑی مشکل سے گزر بسر کرتا ہے۔

    Answer The poor postman makes both ends meet with great difficulty.

Q.9 Change the voice

  1. The door of the car was opened by the driver.

    Answer The driver opened the door of the car.

  2. She likes apples.

    Answer Apples are liked by her.

  3. The snake will have been killed by us.

    Answer We shall have killed the snake.

  4. Why were they beating the boy?

    Answer Why was the boy being beaten by them?

  5. The Board has given me a gold medal.

    Answer I have been given a gold medal by the Board.
