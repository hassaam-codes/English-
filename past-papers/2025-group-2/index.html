Q.8 Translate the following sentences into English

  1. کیا یہ لڑکا امتحان پاس کر چکا ہے؟

    Answer Has this boy passed the examination?

  2. وہ کراچی کب پہنچے؟

    Answer When did they reach Karachi?

  3. پولیس چور کے پیچھے بھاگ رہی ہے۔

    Answer The police are running after the thief.

  4. شریف آدمی کی عزت کی جاتی ہے۔

    Answer A noble man is respected.

  5. جائیداد سب بھائیوں میں تقسیم کر دی گئی۔

    Answer The property was divided among all the brothers.

  6. کیا تم اپنے دوست کو پہچان لو گے؟

    Answer Will you recognise your friend?

  7. مزدوروں نے ہڑتال کر دی ہو گی۔

    Answer The labourers will have gone on strike.

  8. جب میں گھر سے نکلا تو سورج غروب نہیں ہوا تھا۔

    Answer The sun had not set when I left home.

Q.9 Change the voice

  1. The picture is made by the boy.

    Answer The boy makes the picture.

  2. The driver opened the door of the car.

    Answer The door of the car was opened by the driver.

  3. The Board has given me a gold medal.

    Answer I have been given a gold medal by the Board.

  4. We shall have finished our work by March next.

    Answer Our work will have been finished by us by March next.

  5. Why is he mending the chair?

    Answer Why is the chair being mended by him?
